Lesson 4: Basic Sentence Structure

A basic English sentence follows this formula:

Formula: Subject + Verb + Object

  • Subject: Who does the work (e.g., I, He, She, Ram)
  • Verb: Action word (e.g., Eat, Read, Play)
  • Object: Thing receiving the action (e.g., Apple, Book)

Examples:

  • I eat an apple. (म एउटा स्याउ खान्छु।)
  • He reads a book. (ऊ किताब पढ्छ।)
  • She plays football. (उनी फुटबल खेल्छिन्।)

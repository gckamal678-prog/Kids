Lesson 10: Sentence Structure (वाक्य संरचना)

How to build an English sentence using the Golden Formula.

📖 The Golden Formula (सुवर्ण सुत्र)

Subject + Verb + Object

१. Subject (कर्ता / Hero) = काम गर्ने मान्छे (जस्तै: I, He, She, Ram)

२. Verb (क्रिया / Action) = काम वा गतिविधि (जस्तै: eat, play, read)

३. Object (कर्म / Target) = कुन चिजमा काम भयो (जस्तै: apple, football, book)

💡 उदाहरणहरू (Examples):

  • I + eat + an apple. (म स्याउ खान्छु।) 🔊
  • He + plays + football. (ऊ फुटबल खेल्छ।) 🔊
  • She + reads + a book. (उनी किताब पढ्छिन्।) 🔊

Lesson 11: Tenses (समयको ज्ञान)

Tenses show the time of an action.

1. Present Tense (वर्तमान काल)

Actions that happen now or every day.

• Simple Present Tense:

Formula: Subject + Verb (s/es) + Object

  • I go to school. (म स्कुल जान्छु।) 🔊
  • He plays football. (ऊ फुटबल खेल्छ।) 🔊

• Present Continuous Tense:

Formula: Subject + is/am/are + Verb(ing) + Object

  • I am reading a book. (म किताब पढ्दै छु।) 🔊
  • She is singing a song. (उनी गीत गाउँदै छिन्।) 🔊

2. Past Tense (भूत काल)

Actions that already happened in the past.

• Simple Past Tense:

Formula: Subject + Verb (V2) + Object

  • I went to school yesterday. (म हजो स्कुल गएँ।) 🔊
  • He played cricket. (उसले क्रिकेट खेल्यो।) 🔊

• Past Continuous Tense:

Formula: Subject + was/were + Verb(ing) + Object

  • I was eating an apple. (म स्याउ खाँदै थिएँ।) 🔊
  • They were playing. (तिनीहरू खेल्दै थिए।) 🔊

3. Future Tense (भविष्य काल)

Actions that will happen in the coming time.

• Simple Future Tense:

Formula: Subject + will + Verb + Object

  • I will go to Pokhara tomorrow. (म भोलि पोखरा जानेछु।) 🔊
  • She will write a letter. (उनी पत्र लेख्नेछिन्।) 🔊

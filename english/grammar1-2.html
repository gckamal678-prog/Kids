🚀 More Grammar Essentials

💡 Other Core Parts of Speech

  • Adjective (विशेषण): Words that describe nouns or pronouns (e.g., big, red, smart).
  • Verb (क्रिया): Action words or states of being (e.g., run, jump, is, are).
  • Adverb (क्रियाविशेषण): Words that modify verbs or adjectives (e.g., quickly, very, well).
  • Preposition (नामैयोगी): Words that show spatial/temporal relations (e.g., in, on, under, at).
  • Conjunction (संयोजक): Joining words (e.g., and, but, because).
  • Interjection (विस्मयादिबोधक): Exclamatory words for sudden emotion (e.g., Wow!, Oh!, Alas!).

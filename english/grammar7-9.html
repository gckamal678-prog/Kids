Lessons 7, 8 & 9: Prepositions, Conjunctions & Interjections

📚 Lesson 7: Prepositions (नामैयोगी)

📖 What is a Preposition?

Words that show place, position, time, or relationship between words (e.g., in, on, under).

📍 Common Prepositions
📦
In (भित्र)
📘
On (माथि)
🪑
Under (मुनि)
⏰
At (मा/माथि)
🚪
Behind (पछाडि)
↔️
Between (बीचमा)
👀
In front of
☁️
Over (माथि)
🏠
Near (नजिक)

📚 Lesson 8: Conjunctions (संयोजक)

📖 What is a Conjunction?

Words used to connect words, phrases, or clauses together (e.g., and, but, because).

🔗 Common Conjunctions
➕
And (र)
⚡
But (तर)
💡
Because (किनकि)
🔀
Or (वा)
➡️
So (त्यसैले)
⛅
Although (यद्यपि)

📚 Lesson 9: Interjections (विस्मयादिबोधक)

📖 What is an Interjection?

Exclamatory words used to express sudden feelings or emotions (e.g., Wow!, Oh!, Alas!).

🗣️ Common Interjections
🤩
Wow! (वाः!)
😲
Oh! (ओहो!)
😢
Alas! (हाइ-हाइ!)
🎉
Hurrah! (जय हो!)
😣
Ouch! (आः!)
👏
Bravo! (शाबास!)
